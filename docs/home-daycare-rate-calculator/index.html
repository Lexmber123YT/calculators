How this works

Your pay goal plus twelve months of costs is what you need to bring in each year. Divide that by the weeks you bill and you get the weekly need.

Each spot carries a care weight, because babies need more hands: infant 1.25, toddler 1.10, preschool 1.00, school-age 0.60. The weekly need is shared across your spots by weight, at the fullness you plan for. That gives a rate per age group, rounded up to the next $5. These weights are the kit's defaults.

Rates are for a full-time week. The kit also covers part-time and drop-in, and sets your current rates against what you need. This page ignores any current rate, so it shows what you need from scratch.

What the usual free answers miss

  • Parent-side guides show rough local ranges. They don't know what you need to earn.
  • Planning at 100% full is the most common mistake. One open spot for months can erase a raise.
  • Your own take-home pay is a cost. Leaving it out is how a full house can still pay too little.
  • Weeks off with no pay are covered here by billing 52 weeks. Change it if you charge less.
  • Check the answer against your state's child care market rate survey. A rate far above the local market needs a reason families can see.
