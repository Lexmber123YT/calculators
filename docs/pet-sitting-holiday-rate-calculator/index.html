What should I charge for pet sitting over the holidays?

A holiday stay is rarely all holiday. A five-night booking may have two big days, a couple of busy ones and one normal night. This puts each surcharge only on the days it belongs to, then gives you the total and the deposit.

How this works

First the price per night or visit: your normal price plus the price for each extra pet. For a visit service it is multiplied by the visits a day, so every visit on a holiday carries the surcharge.

The normal price for the stay is that price times the nights or days. The surcharge is that same price times each peak day's %, added up: the top holiday days at the top %, the other peak days at the smaller %. Days that are not peak days get no surcharge at all.

The total is the two together. The deposit is your % of the total, rounded to the cent, and the balance is what is left. The kit does the same sum day by day from the peak dates you list; this page asks how many days fall in each level instead.

What the usual free answers miss

  • Many sitters put one holiday rate on the whole booking. Clients notice when a normal Tuesday costs holiday money, and it makes the price harder to defend.
  • Christmas Day and the slow days between the holidays are not the same. Two levels let you charge more for the days you'd most like off.
  • Three visits a day on a holiday means three surcharges. A flat holiday fee per day misses that.
  • Extra pets and the deposit are often worked out by hand after the quote. Here they sit in one number you can send.
