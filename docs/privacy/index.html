Privacy

The calculators

Numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser. They are not sent anywhere or saved. These pages use no cookies, no tracking and no ads.

The free tracker emails

If you sign up for the free tracker, your email address is sent to our email service, Brevo, which stores it and sends our emails. We use it only to send you the tracker and a few short emails about pricing a small business, with an occasional note about our Excel kits. We never sell or share your address.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Click it and you are off the list right away.

The site host

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic server logs (such as your IP address) to keep the service running and safe.
