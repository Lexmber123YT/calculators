You're in. Here's your tracker.

The Real Pay Week tracker is one printable page. Fill it in for one normal week and you will know what your work really pays per hour, the drive and the texts included.

Download the tracker (PDF)

How to use it

  1. Print it. It fits on one sheet of letter paper.
  2. Each day, write each job: what they paid, what it cost you, and your hours.
  3. Count the hours nobody pays for too: the drive, quotes, texts and invoices. That column surprises most people.
  4. At the end of the week, do the seven lines in "Your number". Line R is your real pay per hour.
