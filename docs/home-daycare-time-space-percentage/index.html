How this works

Time % is your business hours in the year divided by the hours in a year (8,760). Business hours are the hours children are here plus the work hours you put in with no children here, times the weeks you provide care.

Space % is the square feet of the rooms used for care divided by the square feet of your whole home. A room used only for the business counts in full. A shared room counts by time %. So your home-cost share is the business-only rooms plus the shared rooms times your time %. With no business-only rooms, that is simply space % times time %.

Your yearly home costs times that share is the part of them counted. The last row shows how much of it comes from the work hours when no children were here.

To keep the page short, some things are held the way the kit starts: the year is a full 365 days (8,760 hours), and every week uses the same two hour numbers. Your real year has holidays, sick days and busy weeks.

What the usual free answers miss

  • Many only multiply two percentages. Rooms used only for the business should not be cut down by time %.
  • Many leave out work done with no children here: cleaning, meal prep, planning, parent calls.
  • Most show a percent and stop. Here you see what it does to your actual home costs.
  • An average week is a guess. Written-down weekly hours are the real thing, and they back up the number if anyone asks.
