How this works

It counts the minutes after your pick-up time. Up to your grace minutes, there is no fee. Past the grace time, the minutes charged start at the end of the grace time. If you choose "Closing time", every minute after your pick-up time is charged once the grace time has passed.

Per minute, each charged minute costs your fee amount. Per block, each block started is charged in full, so 1 minute into a 15-minute block costs the whole block. The fee never goes over your limit. The rule in words is the same sentence the kit puts in its parent notes and policy page.

Held at the kit's usual settings and not on the form: brothers and sisters late on the same day are charged once per family, and any single pick-up can be waived. Those change who pays, not the amount the rule works out.

What the usual free answers miss

  • Most posts give a going rate, such as $1 a minute (rough, many charge more). Few say where the fee starts counting, which is the part parents argue about.
  • A grace time and a fee "from closing time" can look the same on paper but charge very different amounts. The table shows both ways.
  • Per-block fees jump at each block. Check the 15 and 30 minute lines before you print the policy.
  • A limit keeps one bad evening from becoming a big bill, and makes the rule easier to defend.
