Catering quote calculator: how do you price catering per person?

The quick answer is guests times a price per person. That skips the minimum spend, the drive, helper pay, prep and clean-up hours and card fees, so a small party can cost more than it pays. This builds the quote and then checks what it pays you per hour of work.

How this works

The food charge is guests times your price per guest, or your minimum spend if that is more. A travel fee of $2 a mile is added for round-trip miles past the first 30. That is the quote before tax. Sales tax at your rate goes on top, and the deposit is 30% of the total. The per-person price is the quote before tax divided by the guests.

Then the costs: food and packaging for every guest, helper pay at $17 an hour for their hours on site plus 1.5 hours of set-up and clean-up each, the truck at $1.25 a mile, and 3% card and app fees on the total. What is left is your profit. Your own pay is not a cost: it is what is left.

Last, your working hours: hours on site, 3 hours of prep, 1.5 hours of clean-up, and the drive at 35 mph. Profit divided by those hours is what the event pays you per hour. If that is under your target, the page shows the lowest quote before tax (and the per-guest price, rounded up to a quarter) that would reach it. Held at the kit's usual numbers and not on the form: the 30% deposit, 3% card fees, $17 helper pay, 1.5 helper hours, 3 hours prep, 1.5 hours clean-up, 35 mph, $1.25 a mile, 30 free miles, $2 a mile, no rentals or extras.

What the usual free answers miss

  • Dividing food cost by a food cost target (about 25-35%) gives a menu price, not an event price. It leaves out the drive, helper pay and your own hours.
  • Small parties still take a full prep, drive and clean-up. A minimum spend is how the quote covers that; the page shows how many guests it takes to pass it.
  • Card fees are charged on the total with tax, not just the food. Small, but it is on every event.
  • A quote can look fine per person and still pay you less than you need per hour. The last lines show it, with the quote that would fix it.
