Free calculators for people who run a small service business

Most of us set prices by copying the person down the street. These calculators start from your own costs and your own hours instead. Each one answers one question in about thirty seconds.

Cleaning

  • House cleaning What to charge to clean a house

    A price per visit from the home's size, your labor, supplies, drive and a profit margin.

Home daycare

  • Home daycare What to charge for home daycare

    A weekly tuition rate per child that covers your costs and pays you.

  • Home daycare Time-space percentage

    Your time %, space % and the share of home costs that goes with them.

Lawn and home repair

  • Lawn care What to charge to mow a lawn

    A price per visit from yard size, obstacles, drive time and your hourly rate.

  • Handyman What to charge for a small job

    Labor, materials markup and the trip, with a minimum so small jobs still pay.

Lessons, training and photos

  • Tutoring How to price tutoring packages

    Per-lesson price after a package discount, and what you earn per real hour.

  • Personal training Your real hourly rate as a trainer

    What a session pays once unpaid hours, gym fees and travel come out.

  • Photography Your real hourly rate per session

    The session fee spread over every hour: emails, shooting, editing, delivery.

Salons and rentals

  • Booth renter How many clients pay the booth rent

    Your break-even clients a week after product and card fees.

  • Short-term rental Profit per night and break-even occupancy

    What a stay really leaves after the platform fee, the cleaner and supplies.

About these calculators

They come from ClearDayTrackers, a small shop that makes Excel workbooks for one-person businesses. Each calculator uses the same math as one of our kits. The kit does the rest of the job: price sheets, client lists, payment tracking and the scripts for awkward conversations. The calculators are free and stay free.

Nothing you type leaves your browser. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads.

These are rough guides built from common rules of thumb, not advice for your situation. Pages that touch taxes are general bookkeeping help, not tax or legal advice.
