How this works

Add every hour: emails and planning, travel, shooting, gallery delivery, and culling and editing. Take the session fee and subtract the direct costs: mileage, any prints or helper or location fee, and card fees. Divide what is left by all those hours. That is your real hourly rate.

For take-home, the page also takes off your share of yearly business costs and a tax set-aside, then divides by the same hours. The fee that reaches your rate is worked backward from the hours and direct costs.

Three things are held at example values: card fees of 3%, driving at 70 cents a mile, and a rough 28% tax set-aside. In the kit you can change all three, and it can replace your editing guess with your own average once you have logged a few galleries.

What the usual free answers miss

  • They divide by shoot time, or shoot plus editing. Inquiry emails, planning, drive time and gallery delivery are real hours too.
  • They guess the editing time. Your own record of past galleries is more honest than a round number.
  • They ignore direct costs. A 3% card fee and 24 miles of driving come out before you are paid.
  • They stop at the fee. Gear, software and insurance are costs of every session, and tax comes out of what is left.
